# Report style guide

- **Lead with the answer.** The first sentence of the report and of each section states the finding and its number.
- **Name the measure every time**: excess deaths, starvation deaths, total deaths, lost births, population deficit.
- **Ranges, not false precision.** Give the central estimate with its range and what drives the range.
- **Sources inline.** Link the original where it is reachable, with an archived copy for anything at risk. Mark
  anything secondhand or unverified as such.
- **Even-handed.** State the strongest version of every position, including low estimates, before the evidence
  that decides between them. No adjectives doing the work of evidence.
- **Quote sparingly** (under ~25 words per source), paraphrase otherwise.
- **Both languages are complete.** The Chinese report is a full translation of the English one, with the same
  tables, figures and links. Use 万/亿 for counts in Chinese; keep figures identical.
- **Numbers in tables** when there are more than three to compare; charts for time series.
- **No personal data** about contributors, maintainers or private individuals. Reports are credited to the project.
- **Changes are explained.** Every version has `CHANGES.md` listing what moved, why, and which packet items were
  resolved or deferred.
