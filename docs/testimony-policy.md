# Testimony policy / 口述证言政策

Testimony is the most personal material this project receives and the most likely to put people at risk.
These rules apply to every account, published or new.

## What we accept

- First-hand accounts, family accounts from someone who lived through the famine, and second-hand accounts
  (graded T1–T3, see [quality-gates.md](quality-gates.md)).
- Already-published testimony (memoirs, oral-history archives, documentaries): cite it; quote briefly.
- Details that make an account useful as evidence: province and county, season and year, the size of the
  production team or village, numbers of deaths observed, rations, what officials did.

## What we never publish

- Names of living private persons, including the narrator, unless they are already public and agreed.
- Village or production-team names when combined with family details that could identify someone.
- Phone numbers, addresses, ID numbers, emails, photos of faces, voice recordings.
- Names of people accused of wrongdoing who are private individuals, unless already in a published record.

The quality gate scans for emails, phone numbers and ID numbers automatically; reviewers check the rest.

## Consent

The submitter confirms that the narrator (or their family, if the narrator has died) agrees to publication and that
identifying details were removed. Accounts can be withdrawn at any time: open an issue titled "Withdraw <id>" or
ask a maintainer, and the material is removed from the next commit (GitHub history may retain earlier versions;
maintainers will request a purge for sensitive cases).

## Submitting safely

Issues and pull requests are **public and permanent**. If you are in mainland China or have family there:

- Use a separate GitHub account that is not linked to your real identity, and a privacy-preserving connection.
- Leave out anything you would not want associated with you or your family.
- If an account cannot be made safe to publish, do not post it. Maintainers may provide a private channel
  (see the repository's contact information); a privately received account is published only in anonymized form,
  and only with consent.

## How testimony is used

The report uses testimony to illustrate conditions and to check local figures, quoting at most a few sentences.
It never replaces demographic estimation. Contradictions between testimony and statistics are reported as such.

## 中文要点

- **公开且永久：** 议题和拉取请求公开可见。身在中国大陆或家人在国内的提交者，请使用不与真实身份关联的
  GitHub 账号，并只写可以公开的内容。
- **绝不公开：** 在世私人的姓名、村名（与家庭细节结合可识别时）、电话、住址、身份证号、邮箱、照片和录音。
- **同意：** 提交者须确认讲述者（或已故讲述者的家人）同意公开，并已删除可识别信息。可随时申请撤回。
- **用途：** 证言用来说明当时的情况、核对地方数字，引用不超过几句；它不能单独决定全国或省级数字。
