## Summary: deleted vs. surviving

**Deleted at source and surviving only in Wayback or mirrors:**
- **chenqin's Zhihu answer (Q 20207577).** Gone by 2016 (404). The question page showed Zhihu's not-found page by 2023. Wayback holds full-text copies from 2012-05-03 to 2013-05-13, which state about 30 million abnormal deaths.
- **Lin Zhibo's 29 Apr 2012 Weibo denials.** Wayback tried to capture six post URLs on 1–3 May 2012, but every capture is a login redirect, so **no text is archived**. The 2 May 2012 profile capture does preserve his **1 May apology** and shows the denial posts already missing from his feed. The denial wording survives only in press and blog quotes (RFI, China Story).
- **@凤凰台张婉's 37.55 million post (20 May 2013).** Censored on Weibo; Wayback has no capture of the original. The best surviving record is the FreeWeibo page, archived 2014-08-11 with the text, plus the CDT report.
- **ifeng "营养性死亡" article (2013).** Now redirects to the home page. Archived 2013-09-08 to 2016-07-16.
- **炎黄春秋 (yhcqw.com).** Unreachable today. The 2013 no. 12 rebuttal is archived in full (2018–2020) at the post-2016 URL. The 2014 no. 9 article's page frame is archived, but its **body is not**, because it loaded by AJAX.
- **共识网 (21ccom.net).** The whole site is offline. No debate articles by Cao, Yang or Sun were located. Two related pages are archived, one of which went 404 in 2014.

**Still live:**
- NYT Chinese article page for Yang's 2012 rebuttal. The PDF is archived as a real PDF from 2012-09-18; its live status is unclear.
- Both bannedbook.org famine tables. They were never captured under bannedbook.org, but the identical files are archived from 2002 at chinafamine.org and from 2007 at yhcw.net.
- CASS table of contents for Sun's 2011 article. No full text on the official site.
- Jin Hui's 备忘录 on yhcw.net and aisixiang. The earliest archived online copy is chinafamine.org, 2002-10-05.

**Could not determine:** whether Zhihu question 20207577 is live (login wall). The poster's uid for the 凤凰台张婉 post, so the exact weibo.com URL. Any 21ccom page from the core debate. Any pre-2002 online copy of Jin Hui.
