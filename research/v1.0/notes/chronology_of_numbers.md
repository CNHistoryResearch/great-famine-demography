## Q1. Master timeline: each figure's first appearance, producer, method, venue, route to the public, and accessibility

### Takeaway
No national death figure appeared publicly while the famine was under way or in the decade after it. Inside the Party, counts circulated at provincial and prefecture level (1959–62) and in at least one destroyed national survey (1961). Abroad, observers had refugee testimony and intelligence, but no national number. The first national figures came after the 1982 census. In 1981 Sun Yefang published the 1960 death rate (25.4‰). The 1983 Statistical Yearbook published the full vital-rate series and the 1959→1960 population fall of 10 million. Western demographers produced reconstructions in 1984–87 (Coale ~27M, Ashton et al. ~30M, Banister ~30M). On 11 Sept 1984 a State Statistical Bureau spokesman told AP that deaths were "certainly more than 10 million." Since then, estimates have clustered at 15–46M, with ~30M as the modal figure. A minimization camp has been active since 2011.

### Cited Findings (timeline, oldest first)

**Phase 1: internal knowledge and foreign outsiders, 1959–1966**

- **23 May 1959.** The Central Propaganda Department's internal bulletin 《宣教动态》 reported "肿病" (oedema) deaths rather than starvation: "死亡6700多人" across 11 provinces, with Shandong worst hit. It circulated internally and was quoted decades later by Yang Jisheng. — [Yang Jisheng, aisixiang 24241](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/24241.html) (live)
- **3 Sept 1958 / 28 Nov 1960 / 9 Oct 1960.** These are local internal reports quoted by Yang: Qujing prefecture, Yunnan, "死亡18843人"; Huaidian commune, Henan, "死亡12134人"; and a Xinyang/Xi county investigation reporting "共打死、逼死社员1065人" (beatings and forced suicides). — [aisixiang 24241](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/24241.html)
- **15 Dec 1960.** Shandong governor Zhao Jianmin told a provincial Party committee meeting: "全省非正常死亡65万人，外流109万人" (incomplete statistics). This is internal, published later by Yang. — [aisixiang 24241](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/24241.html)
- **Xinyang (Henan) 1960.** In the official count, nine counties had 1960 death rates above 100‰ ("1960年有9个县死亡率超过100‰，为正常年份的好几倍"). This was first published openly in 《当代中国人口》 (1988) and repeated in the 2011 Party History. — [Li Yinchu, Economic Observer, via CDT, 6 July 2011](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/?p=165596) (live). Sun Jingxian quotes the Xinyang prefecture total as "死亡总数436882人", of which "正常死亡155698人". — [Sun Jingxian, aisixiang 71783](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/71783.html) (live). Other accounts put Xinyang deaths at about 1 million. Jin Hui cites Su Xiaokang et al., 《乌托邦祭》 ("饿死近100多万人"). — [Jin Hui, aisixiang 11995](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/11995.html). Yang's count is "超过了100万". — [NYT Chinese review via CDT, Dec 2012](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/?p=267735)
- **4 April 1961. US intelligence (SNIE 13-61).** The estimate "The Economic Situation in Communist China" says: "Widespread famine does not appear to be at hand, but in some provinces many people are now on a bare subsistence diet." It gives no death figure and only warns of "a considerable amount of starvation" if the 1961 harvest is poor. Published in FRUS 1961–63 vol. XXII. — [history.state.gov FRUS d17](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v22/d17) (live)
- **1961. Internal national grain/population survey.** According to Yang Jisheng, Zhou Boping, Chen Guodong and Jia Qiyun, acting for the Ministry of Grain and the State Statistical Bureau, had provinces fill in grain and population tables. The result was "全国人口减少了几千万！", reported only to Zhou Enlai and Mao and then ordered destroyed. — [aisixiang 24241](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/24241.html). **Contested:** Sun Jingxian says Zhou Boping's son denied that any national starvation-death statistics existed. — [aisixiang 71783](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/71783.html)
- **1959–62. Hong Kong consular refugee debriefings.** US consular officer David Dean (Hong Kong 1959–62, 1998 oral history) said "what maybe 1,000 people said in their debriefings" could not be shown to represent the whole country. He also said "analysts back in Washington were believing the Chinese claims," and that "It wasn't until later that the numbers of 30-40 million people dying during this period were confirmed." Ralph Lindstrom: "We didn't have hard numbers." — [ADST, "The Esoteric Art of China Watching during the GLF"](https://adst.org/2015/06/the-esoteric-art-of-china-watching-during-the-great-leap-forward/) (live)
- **1962. Hong Kong refugee exodus.** About 140,000 Chinese entered Hong Kong in 1962, 80,000 of them illegally in a single month. This was driven by the famine and a temporary relaxation of PRC border controls, and it caused a diplomatic crisis. The figures come from a retrospective New York Times article of 3 May 1964, cited on Wikipedia. — [Wikipedia: Refugee wave from the PRC to British Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refugee_wave_from_the_People%27s_Republic_of_China_to_British_Hong_Kong). Dean says that "in 1962 the British Army and the police put up barbed wire to keep people out." — [ADST](https://adst.org/2015/06/the-esoteric-art-of-china-watching-during-the-great-leap-forward/). Refugee eyewitness accounts were "widely dismissed and rarely reported during the famine years." — [ENN Field Exchange summary of Smil, BMJ 1999](https://ennonline.net/fex/10/china)
- **Edgar Snow's denial (1960–62).** Jonathan Mirsky (NYRB, 27 Apr 1989) wrote that Snow "made fun of those who knew there was one" and relied on Rewi Alley's assurances that there was plenty of food. Mirsky also says the famine "was well known in Hong Kong." — [Fairbank & Mirsky, "Mao and Snow," NYRB via ChinaFile](https://www.chinafile.com/library/nyrb-china-archive/mao-and-snow) (live). Snow's own text (*The Other Side of the River*, 1962) was not fetched.
- **Jan–Feb 1962. Seven Thousand Cadres Conference.** Liu Shaoqi presented the "三分天灾,七分人祸" assessment. — [Wikipedia: Great Chinese Famine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Chinese_Famine). Liu's remark to Mao is quoted by Yang Jisheng as "饿死这么多人，历史上要写你我的" (1962). — [Yang, 炎黄春秋 2014 no. 9, via CDT](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/363046.html). Meng Lei (Economic Observer) quotes the fuller form "饿死这么多人，历史要写上你我的，人相食，要上书的！" and places it at the 七千人大会, without a source. — [CDT, 10 May 2012](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/?p=225668). **Not verified:** the primary source and exact date of the remark. Accounts differ on whether it was said at the conference or in summer 1962.
- **June 1966. Official external line.** Zhou Enlai told the Albanian leadership: "for about three years in a row, we suffered heavy damage due to great natural disasters." — [Wilson Center Digital Archive doc 117703](https://digitalarchive.wilsoncenter.org/document/117703) (live)

**Phase 2: opening of data, 1979–1984**

- **25 Nov 1980.** The restricted journal 《农业经济丛刊》 published a study of Liyuan commune, Anhui: population fell from 5,730 (1957) to 2,870 (1961), and "More than half died of starvation [e si] or fled the area." It used the rare term 饿死. — [NYRB, "Starving to Death in China," 16 June 1983](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/1983/06/16/starving-to-death-in-china/) (live; author's name not captured in my fetch)
- **15 Feb 1981. Sun Yefang (孙冶方).** In 《经济管理》 (*Jingji Guanli*) no. 2, p. 3, Sun wrote of the "high price in blood" of the Leap and said "the national death rate rose from 10.8 per 1,000 in 1957 to 25.4 per 1,000 in 1960" (NYRB translation). The NYRB author inferred about 9 million extra deaths in 1960 alone; that is the author's inference, not Sun's figure. This is the earliest *open* PRC publication of the famine-year death rate I could verify. — [NYRB 1983](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/1983/06/16/starving-to-death-in-china/). **Not verified:** the Chinese title of Sun's article and whether it also stated the 10-million population fall. The suggestion that it appeared in 学习与探索 is unsupported; the NYRB cites 经济管理.
- **27 June 1981. CCP Resolution on Party History.** Section 17 says the errors of the Great Leap Forward and the anti-"Right opportunism" struggle, together with natural disasters and the Soviet scrapping of contracts, caused serious economic difficulties in 1959–61 and "serious losses to our country and people." It gives no death figure. — [English text PDF, marxistarkiv.se](https://www.marxistarkiv.se/kina/engelska/on_cpc_history.pdf) (live). According to Wikipedia, the official label shifted from 三年自然灾害 to 三年困难时期 after this resolution. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Chinese_Famine). zh-Wikipedia says the change came "in the 1980s". — [zh.wikipedia 三年困难时期](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/三年困难时期) (live)
- **1982 census, then the 1983 China Statistical Yearbook (中国统计年鉴 1983).** The NBS, under director Li Chengrui and with State Council approval, published for the first time the household-registration series from 1949. It shows 1959 = 67,207万 and 1960 = 66,207万, a fall of "一千万". — [Yang, aisixiang 24241](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/24241.html); [Yang, YHCQ 2014 via CDT](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/363046.html). The 1983 yearbook (p. 105) was the first uninterrupted official vital-rates series since 1949. The official 1960 crude death rate is 25.4‰ (ZTN 1984 p. 83 also cited). — [Scharping 1986, Kölner China-Studien WP 1986-1](https://www.uni-koeln.de/phil-fak/ostas/moderne/papers/No%201986-1.pdf) (live). Later yearbooks reused the same figures. — [Jin Hui 1993, aisixiang 11995](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/11995.html). The same table (1960 CDR 25.43‰, NIR −4.57‰) is reproduced in the 2012 chenqin Zhihu answer from 《新中国50/55/60年统计资料汇编》 (archived copy: see data/registry/archive_snapshots.yaml, ARC-ZHIHU-CHENQIN).
- **1983. State Family Planning Commission 1‰ fertility sample survey (1982 survey).** It was later used by Coale, by Jiang Zhenghua and, in the 2010s, by Yang Songlin. — [Yang, YHCQ 2014](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/363046.html); [Hill, World Bank 1988](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/492811468771874832/pdf/Demographic-Trends-in-China-from-1950-to-1982.pdf)
- **1984. Ansley Coale, *Rapid Population Change in China, 1952–1982* (National Academy Press).** Coale combined the census with the 1982 fertility survey. His adjusted rates give about 27M excess deaths, 15M of them in 1960. — [Scharping 1986](https://www.uni-koeln.de/phil-fak/ostas/moderne/papers/No%201986-1.pdf). Another summary gives 24.81M for 1959–61 and 26.8M for 1958–63. — [IUSSP paper on population change from the great famine](https://iussp.org/sites/default/files/event_call_for_papers/A%20study%20of%20population%20change%20led%20by%20China’s%20great%20famine_.pdf)
- **Dec 1984. Ashton, Hill, Piazza & Zeitz, "Famine in China, 1958–61," *PDR* 10(4): 613–645.** About 30M. — [Wikipedia citations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Chinese_Famine)
- **11 Sept 1984. First official Chinese national death statement.** AP (Jeff Bradley, Peking) reported that SSB spokesman Xu Gang said "over 10 million people died of unnatural death due to both man-made factors and serious natural disasters" in 1959–62, "certainly more than 10 million." The same story noted US estimates of 27M (Coale) to 30M (Banister), and that official media had previously spoken only of "natural disasters." — [AP wire text preserved in John McCarthy's SAIL archive, FAMINE.NS[E84,JMC]](https://www.saildart.org/FAMINE.NS[E84,JMC]) (live)
- **1985, IUSSP conference, Florence.** Judith Banister estimated 29.87M excess deaths and Gérard Calot 28.51M for 1958–62. — [IUSSP paper](https://iussp.org/sites/default/files/event_call_for_papers/A%20study%20of%20population%20change%20led%20by%20China’s%20great%20famine_.pdf). NBS head Li Chengrui presented Chinese data there and discussed them with foreign scholars. — [Yang, YHCQ 2014](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/363046.html)
- **1986. Thomas Scharping.** Official vital rates alone imply about 16–17M excess deaths for 1958–61. — [Scharping 1986](https://www.uni-koeln.de/phil-fak/ostas/moderne/papers/No%201986-1.pdf)

**Phase 3: semi-official Chinese and Western reconstructions, 1986–2010**

- **1986/87. Jiang Zhenghua (蒋正华) & Li Nan.** NBS and the State Family Planning Commission commissioned the study. Using life tables, it found 17M non-normal deaths ("非正常死亡1700万"). It was published in 《西安交通大学学报》 1986 no. 3 and 《中国人口年鉴1987》, and endorsed by a 1987 State Council census office expert review. — [Yang, YHCQ 2014](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/363046.html); [IUSSP refs](https://iussp.org/sites/default/files/event_call_for_papers/A%20study%20of%20population%20change%20led%20by%20China’s%20great%20famine_.pdf). Banister called the survey flawed because many deaths went unrecorded. — [McGregor, FT, 12 June 2010, CDT translation](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/?p=79809)
- **1987. Banister, *China's Changing Population*.** About 30M. — [Wikipedia table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Chinese_Famine); contradicted by [zh.wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/三年困难时期), which lists Banister 1987 at about 23M (likely a zh-wiki error).
- **1987. Peng Xizhe, *PDR*.** About 23M, based on 14 provinces. — [Wikipedia table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Chinese_Famine)
- **1988.** Luo Sheng's University of Pennsylvania dissertation estimates 28.89M for 1959–63. — [IUSSP](https://iussp.org/sites/default/files/event_call_for_papers/A%20study%20of%20population%20change%20led%20by%20China’s%20great%20famine_.pdf). Kenneth Hill, World Bank Discussion Paper 22 (Feb 1988): "This demographic crisis cost some 30 million premature deaths." — [World Bank PDF](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/492811468771874832/pdf/Demographic-Trends-in-China-from-1950-to-1982.pdf) (live)
- **Smil's summary.** "the first calculations by American demographers put the toll at between 16.6 and 23 million"; "More detailed later studies came up with 23 to 30 million". — [ENN/Smil BMJ 1999](https://ennonline.net/fex/10/china)
- **1989. Chinese Academy of Sciences, 《生存与发展》 (Science Press), p. 39:** "按保守的估计，因营养不足而死亡约1500万人". — [Jin Hui, aisixiang 11995](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/11995.html)
- **Mid/late 1980s, leaked 1990s. Chen Yizi (陈一谘), System Reform Institute.** Chen reported 43–46M from a team of about 200 that examined internal Party records, county by county in five provinces, extrapolated nationally. — [Wikipedia: Chen Yizi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chen_Yizi). Daniel Southerland, Washington Post (reprinted in Moscow Times, 22 July 1994): "Chen Yizi of Princeton University did research for years in China and determined that 43 million had died in the famine," and "a figure recently matched by a report from a think tank in Shanghai." — [Moscow Times archive](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/archive/reports-tell-horror-of-maos-policies) (live). zh-Wikipedia cites Becker (1996/98) and Dikötter (2010) for 43–46M, plus "another internal document" giving 50–60M. — [zh.wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/三年困难时期). Methods were called unknown or unverifiable (Riskin, Ó Gráda). Former institute director Wang Xiaoqiang denied the project existed when asked by Yang Songlin. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Chinese_Famine)
- **1991. Ding Shu (丁抒), 《人祸：大跃进与大饥荒》, 九十年代杂志社, Hong Kong.** Described as the first monograph on the famine. — [minjian-danganguan archive 466](https://minjian-danganguan.org/archive/466). Ding's lowest figure is 35M per Yang Jisheng's 2010 speech. — [CDT ?p=103671](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/?p=103671). The Wikipedia table gives 35–44M (1997 edition). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Chinese_Famine)
- **1991. Hu Sheng (ed.), 《中国共产党的七十年》.** Printed the 10-million 1960 population decline before the 2011 Party History did. — [Economic Observer via CDT](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/?p=165596)
- **1992. Li Rui, 《毛泽东的早年与晚年》 (Guizhou People's Press), p. 160:** "饿死成千万人，这是古今中外历史上承平之世绝无仅有之事。" — [Jin Hui, aisixiang 11995](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/11995.html)
- **1993. Jin Hui (金辉), "'三年自然灾害'备忘录," 《社会》 (Shanghai) nos. 4–5.** His estimates are at least 27.91M for 1959–61 and possibly 34.71M rural; the conclusion reads "非正常死亡人数可能高达4060万". — [aisixiang 11995](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/11995.html) (live)
- **8 Apr 1993. Liu Binyan, NYRB:** the famine "snuffed out more than 30 million lives." — [NYRB](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/1993/04/08/an-unnatural-disaster/)
- **1996. Jasper Becker, *Hungry Ghosts*:** 30–60M. — [Wikipedia table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Chinese_Famine)
- **1997. Li Chengrui (李成瑞, former NBS director), "'大跃进'引起的人口变动," 《中共党史研究》 1997 no. 2, pp. 1–14.** He revised Coale to 22M non-normal deaths and judged both Coale's and Jiang's data "具有高度的可靠性". — [Yang, YHCQ 2014](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/363046.html); [IUSSP refs](https://iussp.org/sites/default/files/event_call_for_papers/A%20study%20of%20population%20change%20led%20by%20China’s%20great%20famine_.pdf). The Central Party History Research Office reviewed the work in 1997. — [Meng Lei via CDT](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/?p=225668)
- **1990s. Wang Weizhi (王维志), ex-Ministry of Public Security demographer.** Estimated 33–35M. — [Yang 2010 speech, CDT](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/?p=103671); [McGregor FT 2010 via CDT](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/?p=79809). Zhang Qingwu, former head of the MPS household registration office: "我个人估计2000多万吧". — [CDT, Sept 2012](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/252335.html)
- **NBS internal figure (date unknown).** "非正常死亡人数1619．92万人，少出生3150万人", as cited by Yang in an Aug 2010 lecture. — [CDT ?p=103671](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/?p=103671). The original document was not found.
- **1999. Vaclav Smil, *BMJ* 319:1619:** about 30M. — [ENN](https://ennonline.net/fex/10/china)
- **2005. Cao Shuji (曹树基), 《大饥荒：1959–1961年的中国人口》 (Hong Kong: 时代国际) and 《中国人口科学》 2005 no. 1.** Based on prefecture-level gazetteer data: 32.458M. — [IUSSP](https://iussp.org/sites/default/files/event_call_for_papers/A%20study%20of%20population%20change%20led%20by%20China’s%20great%20famine_.pdf); [Yang YHCQ 2014](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/363046.html)
- **2008. Lin Yunhui (林蕴晖), 《中华人民共和国史》 vol. 4 (CUHK):** 21.44M for 1959–61, near 30M counting from 1958. — [zh.wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/三年困难时期). In 2013 he told the NYT: "about 30 million abnormal deaths." — [MCLC repost of NYT, 16 Oct 2013](https://lists.osu.edu/pipermail/mclc/2013-October/002361.html)
- **2008. Yang Jisheng (杨继绳), 《墓碑》, 2 vols, Hong Kong 天地图书 (Cosmos Books).** 36M non-normal deaths plus 40M lost births ("中国人口损失约7600万"). He took the midpoint of demographic studies, checked against provincial archives ("I consulted twelve provincial archives and the central archives"). — [aisixiang 24241](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/24241.html); [Yang YHCQ 2014](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/363046.html); [ChinaFile/NYRB "Finding the Facts About Mao's Victims"](https://www.chinafile.com/library/nyrb-china-archive/finding-facts-about-maos-victims). The minjian-danganguan record lists 2009 (likely a later printing). — [minjian-danganguan 458](https://www.minjian-danganguan.org/s/minjian-danganguan/item/458) (PDF download available). English edition: FSG, 2012.
- **2010. Frank Dikötter, *Mao's Great Famine* (Bloomsbury/Walker).** At least 45M. He adds 50% to Cao's 32.5M, which Ó Gráda criticises as based on an implausibly low baseline death rate (10‰ vs his "probably about 25 per thousand"). — [Ó Gráda, UCD WP11/03 (published in PDR 37(1), 2011)](https://www.ucd.ie/economics/t4media/WP11_03.pdf) (live)
- **2010. Sun Shanggong (Peking U.):** about 44M (does not separate fewer births from extra deaths). — [zh.wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/三年困难时期)

**Phase 4: official texts since 2011, and the minimization camp**

- **Jan 2011. 《中国共产党历史》第二卷 (Party History Research Office).** It says "据正式统计，1960年全国总人口比上年减少1000万" and mentions Xinyang's nine counties above 100‰. It gives no death total ("采取了回避的态度", per VOA). — [VOA 5 July 2011 via CDT](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/166472.html); [Economic Observer via CDT](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/?p=165596). Note: some commentators misreport this as "非正常死亡1000万" (e.g. [Meng Lei](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/?p=225668)). The text actually gives a net population decline.
- **2011. Sun Jingxian (孙经先), "关于我国20世纪60年代人口变动问题的研究," 《马克思主义研究》 2011 no. 6 (CASS).** He called the claim of "数千万人非正常死亡" a "重大谣言" and attributed the drop to unregistered migration. — [CDT, 17 Sept 2012](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/252335.html). Wikipedia gives his estimate as 3.66M. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Chinese_Famine)
- **2011. Mao Yushi:** 36M (Cato 2014 version). — [Wikipedia table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Chinese_Famine)
- **29–30 Apr 2012. Lin Zhibo (林治波, People's Daily) on Weibo:** "有人为了糟蹋毛主席，竟然夸张污蔑1960-1962年饿死几千万人". After a backlash he partly retracted on 30 April, accepting the 1960 population-fall data, and deleted the posts. — [CDT/RFI, 30 Apr 2012](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/223180.html) (live). The date is **2012, not 2013**. The Zhihu answer by "chenqin" explicitly responds to "林治波微博". It argues that about 30M excess deaths has been settled since the 1982 census, and that a 29.57M deficit among people aged 40+ (1964 census vs counterfactual) cannot be explained by fewer births (user screenshots 91701c8e, f305db9a, 27a5011b).
- **Sept 2012.** Yang's rebuttal of Sun, 《脱离实际必然走向谬误》, appeared on NYT Chinese. — [CDT 252335](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/252335.html)
- **20 May 2013.** A Weibo post (凤凰台张婉) citing 37.55M and quoting the Party History was deleted (FreeWeibo / CDT River Crab Archive). — [CDT English, June 2013](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/2013/06/river-crab-archive-the-death-toll-from-maos-great-famine/)
- **Early Sept 2013.** Sun Jingxian claimed at most about 2.5M "营养性死亡", which became the top Weibo search term on 6 Sept 2013. — [Global Voices, 7 Sept 2013](https://globalvoices.org/2013/09/07/dont-say-millions-starved-during-chinas-great-famine-try-nutritional-death-instead/). He also wrote in the Global Times. — [NYT 16 Oct 2013 via MCLC](https://lists.osu.edu/pipermail/mclc/2013-October/002361.html)
- **2013. Yang Songlin (杨松林), 《总要有人说出真相》:** at most about 4M "abnormal fatalities"; Wikipedia gives 3.5–4M, based on the household-registration vs fertility-survey discrepancy. — [MCLC/NYT](https://lists.osu.edu/pipermail/mclc/2013-October/002361.html); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Chinese_Famine)
- **2013–14.** Yang published 《驳"饿死三千万是谣言"》 (炎黄春秋 2013) and 《关于大饥荒年代人口损失的讨论》 (炎黄春秋 2014 no. 9). Sun replied in 《学习》 (2013) and 《红旗文稿》 (2014). Per Yang, Sun acknowledged 316万 starvation deaths at a July 2014 Wuhan conference. — [CDT 363046](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/363046.html); [Sun, aisixiang 71783](https://www.aisixiang.com/data/71783.html)
- **22 Sept 2014.** A CASS article, carried by Xinhua, People's Daily and China News Service, accused "hostile Western forces" of exaggerating the toll. — [Chinese PEN/FP, Bethany Allen-Ebrahimian, 25 Sept 2014](https://www.chinesepen.org/english/?p=3469); [chinanews.com.cn 2014-09-22 original](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/gn/2014/09-22/6614931.shtml) (live but encoding garbled in fetch)
- **2016. Mi Hong & Jia Ning, 《人口研究》 40(1):22–37 (modified Lee-Carter):** 16.24–23.37M, medium ≤19.8M. — [rkyj.ruc.edu.cn](https://rkyj.ruc.edu.cn/CN/Y2016/V40/I1/22) (live)
- **2017. Cheng Enfu & Zhan Zhihua:** 4–5M. — [Wikipedia table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Chinese_Famine)
- **Nov 2021. Third history resolution:** "遗憾的是，党的八大形成的正确路线未能完全坚持下去，先后出现'大跃进'运动、人民公社化运动等错误，反右派斗争也被严重扩大化。" It does not mention 1959–61 "serious losses" or any deaths. — [PRC Embassy Indonesia full text](https://id.china-embassy.gov.cn/ztbd/jrzg/202111/t20211118_10450069.htm) (live)

### Inferences
- The chain of transmission runs as follows. Internal Party counts (1959–62) stayed secret. Refugee testimony in Hong Kong gave qualitative evidence but no national number. The first quantitative leakage came through *official* openness: Sun Yefang's 1981 death rate and the 1983 yearbook. Western demographers turned those data into national totals in 1984. The PRC's own first public national figure ("more than 10 million", AP, Sept 1984) was a reactive answer to those Western estimates. Chinese-commissioned work (Jiang 17M; Li Chengrui 22M) was the semi-official reply.
- Chen Yizi's 43–46M is the main "insider leak" figure. It reached the West through émigré channels after 1989 (Washington Post 1994; Becker 1996) and has never been documented.

- The NYRB 1983 article's author (byline not captured).

## Q2. What was the very first public number for national deaths, and who produced it?

### Takeaway
I found no public national death number from any source between 1959 and 1980. Contemporaneous Western intelligence (SNIE 1961) gave none, and Hong Kong consular staff said "We didn't have hard numbers." The first published *national* data were Sun Yefang's 1960 death rate (Feb 1981) and the 1983 yearbook series. The first published national excess-death *totals* were Western demographic reconstructions: Coale 1984 (~27M) and Ashton et al. 1984 (~30M), with earlier "first calculations" of 16.6–23M per Smil. The first *official PRC* national death statement was the SSB's "more than 10 million" to AP on 11 Sept 1984.

### Cited Findings
- SNIE 13-61 (4 Apr 1961) contains no death figure. — [FRUS](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v22/d17)
- "We didn't have hard numbers" (Lindstrom); numbers were confirmed "later" (Dean). — [ADST](https://adst.org/2015/06/the-esoteric-art-of-china-watching-during-the-great-leap-forward/)
- Sun Yefang, 《经济管理》 no. 2, 15 Feb 1981: death rate 10.8‰ (1957) → 25.4‰ (1960). — [NYRB 1983](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/1983/06/16/starving-to-death-in-china/)
- "The true extent of the famine was not revealed to the world until the publication of age distribution data" from the 1982 census; "the first calculations by American demographers put the toll at between 16.6 and 23 million". — [ENN/Smil](https://ennonline.net/fex/10/china)
- SSB spokesman Xu Gang (AP, 11 Sept 1984): "over 10 million people died of unnatural death... certainly more than 10 million"; official media had previously cited only "natural disasters". — [saildart AP copy](https://www.saildart.org/FAMINE.NS[E84,JMC])
- "The government has never publicly stated a death toll" (McGregor 2010). This conflicts with the 1984 AP quote, which was a spokesman's verbal statement rather than a published figure. — [CDT FT translation](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/?p=79809)

### Inferences
- The "first public number" depends on definition. For a death rate, it is Sun Yefang in 1981. For an official population decline, it is the NBS 1983 yearbook (10M). For a national excess-death total, it is Western demographers in 1984 (the earliest is probably the 16.6–23M "first calculations", authors unverified). For an official PRC verbal statement, it is the SSB "more than 10 million" in 1984.

### Gaps
- Who exactly produced the "16.6–23M" first calculations (possibly Aird or Coale 1981–83). Not verified.
- Any 1960s Taiwanese/KMT or Hong Kong press national figures. I found none in this session, though they may exist in period newspapers.

## Q3. When did numbers from internal Chinese sources first leak abroad, and through whom?

### Takeaway
Internal counts came out in waves. (a) In the early 1980s, restricted-circulation journals such as 《农业经济丛刊》 (1980) were obtained and translated abroad. (b) Li Chengrui's NBS presented data to foreign scholars at the 1985 Florence IUSSP conference. (c) After 1989, émigrés such as Chen Yizi (43–46M, reported 1994) spoke out. (d) Provincial archive figures were published in Hong Kong by Yang Jisheng (2008) and taken from archives by Dikötter (2010).

### Cited Findings
- The restricted 《农业经济丛刊》 (25 Nov 1980) Liyuan commune study was translated in the NYRB in 1983. — [NYRB](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/1983/06/16/starving-to-death-in-china/)
- Li Chengrui at Florence 1985 discussed data with foreign scholars. — [Yang YHCQ 2014](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/363046.html)
- Chen Yizi: 43M (Washington Post/Moscow Times, July 1994); 43–46M per Wikipedia and zh-wiki citing Becker. — [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/archive/reports-tell-horror-of-maos-policies); [Wikipedia Chen Yizi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chen_Yizi)
- An unpublished Chinese government study (Jiang) "admits to 17 million unnatural deaths." — [Foreign Policy, "Let Them Eat Grass," 2013](https://foreignpolicy.com/2013/01/02/let-them-eat-grass/); "unpublished Chinese materials hint at totals closer to 40 million" (Smil 1999). — [ENN](https://ennonline.net/fex/10/china)
- Yang Jisheng took provincial archive material out under cover of studying grain history, helped by about 20 insiders, and published in Hong Kong in 2008. — [McGregor/FT via CDT](https://chinadigitaltimes.net/chinese/?p=79809)

### Inferences
- Before 1989, "leaks" were mostly sanctioned releases of data by the reformist NBS and a few restricted journals. After 1989, émigrés became the channel for the highest figures.

### Gaps
- Chen Yizi's documentation has never been published; Wang Xiaoqiang denied the study existed ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Chinese_Famine)).

## Q4. How did the "widely accepted" range (~15–45M, modal ~30M) form?

### Takeaway
The range has three layers. The floor (15–17M) comes from official vital rates (Scharping 16–17M), Jiang Zhenghua's commissioned 17M, the CAS 1989 15M, and the NBS internal 16.2M. The modal ~30M comes from the 1984–88 Western census reconstructions (Coale 27M, Ashton 30M, Banister 30M, Calot 28.5M, Hill 30M), later confirmed by Cao Shuji (32.5M) and Yang (36M). The ceiling (40–46M+) comes from Chen Yizi, Jin Hui, Ding Shu and Dikötter. The 22M of Li Chengrui (1997) is the highest figure printed in an official Party journal.

### Cited Findings
- Figures and sources as listed in Q1: [Scharping](https://www.uni-koeln.de/phil-fak/ostas/moderne/papers/No%201986-1.pdf); [IUSSP](https://iussp.org/sites/default/files/event_call_for_papers/A%20study%20of%20population%20change%20led%20by%20China’s%20great%20famine_.pdf); [Hill/World Bank](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/492811468771874832/pdf/Demographic-Trends-in-China-from-1950-to-1982.pdf); [Wikipedia table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Chinese_Famine)
- Ó Gráda: demographers' range was "between 18 million and 32.5 million"; Dikötter's 45M is the outlier. — [UCD WP11/03](https://www.ucd.ie/economics/t4media/WP11_03.pdf)
- NYT 2013: "Mainstream scholars estimate 20 to 30 million or more"; scholarly range about 17–45M. — [MCLC](https://lists.osu.edu/pipermail/mclc/2013-October/002361.html)
- Lin Yunhui (retired party historian, National Defense University): "about 30 million". — [MCLC](https://lists.osu.edu/pipermail/mclc/2013-October/002361.html)

### Inferences
- The spread comes mostly from (a) the assumed "normal" baseline death rate (Ó Gráda) and (b) whether registration undercounts are corrected. The chenqin Zhihu answer makes the same point with age-structure data.

### Gaps
- No single document records when "30 million" became the journalistic default. Liu Binyan was already using ">30 million" in 1993.

## Q5. Denial/minimization camp and censorship: what is gone and what survives

### Takeaway
Since 2011 a minimization camp has been published in Party-run venues: Sun Jingxian (马克思主义研究, 学习, 红旗文稿, Global Times; 2.5–3.66M), Yang Songlin (2013; ≤4M), the CASS 2014 "hostile Western forces" piece, and Cheng Enfu/Zhan Zhihua (2017; 4–5M). Over the same period, critical venues were shut or censored. 《墓碑》 is banned on the mainland. Yang was forced out as 炎黄春秋 editor in 2015, and the magazine ceased publication on 17 July 2016. Weibo posts citing tens of millions have been deleted.
