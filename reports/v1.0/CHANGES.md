# v1.0 (2026-11-01): initial release

First version of the report, produced from five parallel research strands (national modeling, age and
urban–rural analysis, regional records, chronology of the numbers, critique of published methods), one
supplementary provincial data search, and a Wayback Machine check of deleted sources.

- National estimate: ~31 million excess deaths (mid-1958 to mid-1963), range 22–40 million, from a
  census-calibrated cohort-component model (`models/national/`).
- Answered questions Q-0001 to Q-0008 (see `questions/questions.yaml`).
- Open research backlog carried forward as accepted questions Q-0009 to Q-0014.
- Notes: `research/v1.0/notes/`. Registries seeded by `pipeline/tools/seed_registry_v1.py`.
