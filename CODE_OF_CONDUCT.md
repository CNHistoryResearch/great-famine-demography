# Code of conduct

This project documents a catastrophe in which tens of millions of people died. Many contributors are descendants
of victims or survivors. Behave accordingly.

- Argue about evidence, not about people. Assume good faith; ask for sources, not motives.
- No harassment, slurs, doxxing, or attempts to identify anonymous contributors or testimony narrators.
- No denial-as-trolling or atrocity-as-entertainment. Low and high estimates are welcome when they come with evidence.
- Respect testimony. Do not mock, demand proof from, or pressure people who share family accounts.

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, lock threads, and block accounts that break these rules. Report problems
to the maintainers listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS`. Based on the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
